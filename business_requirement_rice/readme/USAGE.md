To score a requirement:

1. Go to *Business Requirements* and open or create a requirement.
2. Go to the *RICE* tab and fill in reach, impact, confidence and effort.
3. The score is computed as you fill them in.

To prioritise your requirements, go to the list view and sort by the *RICE
Score* column, or use the *Scored* filter to leave out the requirements nobody
has estimated yet. Reach, impact, confidence and effort are all optional: a
requirement nobody has estimated simply scores zero.

Requirements with an effort of zero score zero, so an unestimated effort never
sends a requirement to the top of the list. A negative reach or effort scores
zero as well, since neither is a usable estimate.
