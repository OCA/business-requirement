This module links project tasks to business requirements, so you can
follow from a requirement the work done to fulfil it.

- Tasks get a *Business Requirement* field, which can be searched and
  grouped by.
- Business requirements get a *Tasks* smart button listing their tasks.
  Tasks created from there are linked to the requirement.

Usage
=====

To link a task to a business requirement:

1. Go to *Project* and open a task.
2. Set the *Business Requirement* field.

To see the tasks of a business requirement:

1. Go to *Business Requirements* and open a requirement.
2. Click on the *Tasks* smart button. New tasks created from this list
   are linked to the requirement.

The *Business Requirement* field is only shown to Business Requirement
users, and the *Tasks* smart button only to Project users.
