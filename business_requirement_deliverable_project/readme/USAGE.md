To create the tasks of a business requirement:

1.  Go to *Business Requirements* and open a draft or confirmed
    requirement.
2.  In *On Approval*, choose what to do with its deliverables:
    - *Nothing*: no task is created.
    - *Create tasks in a project*: choose the *Project* in which to
      create them.
    - *Create a new project with tasks*: a project named after the
      requirement is created when it is approved.
3.  Approve the requirement, or move it straight to *In progress* or
    *Done*. A task is created for each deliverable.

If deliverables are added after the approval, click on *Create Tasks*
to create their tasks. Deliverables which already have a task, even an
archived one, don't get a new one.

To see the tasks of a business requirement, click on its *Tasks* smart
button. New tasks created from this list are linked to the requirement.

The *On Approval* and *Project* fields, and the *Tasks* smart button,
are only shown to Project users. Tasks are created on approval anyway.
